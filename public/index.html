01 — THE SYSTEM

Inside the runtime.

Selection, motion and input have different jobs. Each section exposes its own controls, with shared checks to keep their behavior consistent.

01

Selection & prediction

Selection starts with the character’s body parts and their orientation. Prediction projects measured XYZ movement forward in time; vertical compensation treats jumping and falling separately from lateral motion. Auto refreshes the timing baseline, while a manual value supplies a starting point in seconds.

02

Coordinated inputs

Silent Aim selects a submitted point, Aim Assist follows a point, and Triggerbot generates shooting input. Hold and toggle activation remain independent, even with shared bindings. Target checks determine eligibility before the game’s weapon cooldown controls firing.

03

A readable visual layer

ESP presents player names and selection outlines. Drawing visibility is separate from target eligibility. Weapon profiles choose Height and Width at Close, Medium and Far thresholds; online whitelist assignments decide who is eligible.

04

Geometry & shot paths

The camera and muzzle occupy different positions. Screen-space selection and world-space shot checks use different units: pixels describe the view, while studs describe the world. AntiCurve evaluates the candidate path; prediction moves the candidate point. Neither changes the server’s hit rules.
