Make it work for you.

Choose how you aim, what you see and when each feature runs. Keep the settings you use, and leave the rest off.

Silent Aim

Choose a body part or the closest point inside it. Adjust how quickly that point follows movement, how close it gets to the edges, and which checks it needs to pass.

Movement prediction

Prediction follows movement across X, Y and Z, with separate handling for vertical lead. Let Auto choose the timing baseline, or enter your own. The resolver uses measured movement when reported velocity is unreliable.

Aim Assist & Triggerbot

Aim Assist follows your chosen point; Triggerbot handles shooting input. Separate hold and toggle bindings let the features work together while preserving their own eligibility checks.

Shot checks

The camera and muzzle have different origins. AntiCurve offers 3D-angle and Endmath checks, alongside configurable wall and ground checks. Each has its own job in deciding whether a shot path is usable.

Player visuals

Plain outlines, readable names and smaller emoji labels. Visibility controls remain separate from targeting. Labels are cached and drawing objects are reused to cut down on repeated work.

Your setup

Per-weapon ranges and selection dimensions, shared checks and independent activation. Save one config to your Discord account and apply changes through the dashboard without rebuilding your script.

Before you start

A few things to know.

Keys, compatibility and getting started.

What is neoperro?

Neoperro is a Lua script. Use it with your own settings, or manage your saved config from the dashboard.

What can I do in the dashboard?

Sign in with Discord, link your license, edit one saved config and check its delivery to a connected client. Your license page shows its current status, expiry and device-reset availability.

How long does a license last?

The duration depends on the license you purchase. Check the offer before paying and use the License tab to see its current expiry. Website sign-in does not extend a license.

Can I share or resell my key?

No. A key is for one person and cannot be shared. Once used or linked to a Discord account, it cannot be resold or transferred. Confirmed sharing or unauthorized resale may lead to the license being blacklisted. See the Terms of Service for details.

Can I reset my device?

Eligible HWID-bound licenses can reset their device binding once every 24 hours. The panel shows when the next reset is available. A device reset does not change the linked Discord account.

Which clients and games are supported?

Compatibility depends on the current runtime, game and client version. Check the current product information before buying. An untested client or a third-party update may require changes.

Will it affect performance?

Performance depends on your device, enabled features and the current game. We work on reducing overhead, but do not promise zero impact or a fixed frame rate.

What happens when something breaks?

Email support@fosternya.xyz with the version, what happened and steps to reproduce it. Leave out keys, connection tokens and passwords. Fixes depend on the cause; there is no guaranteed same-day schedule.

What is the refund policy?

Purchases are generally final once access has been supplied, to the extent permitted by law. Any mandatory refund, withdrawal or consumer rights still apply. Read the purchase terms before paying.
