What’s included.

Pick what you use.
Set it up your way.

Silent Aim

Choose a body part or the closest point inside it. Adjust how the point moves and how much room it keeps from the edges.

Prediction

Follow movement in every direction. Auto updates the timing for you, or you can set a value yourself. Jumping and falling have their own controls.

Aim Assist & Triggerbot

Smooth aim movement and automatic shooting, with separate keybinds. Use hold or toggle, and choose which checks each feature follows.

Visuals

Simple player names and outlines. Choose what shows, change the colors, and keep friends easy to spot.

Weapon settings

Set different box sizes for each weapon at close, medium and far range. Switch weapons and the matching settings take over.

Live config

Keep one saved config. Send changes to your linked client, or export a copy to keep on your device.
