03 / FAQ

Good to know.

Answers about access, configs, and compatibility.

What do I need to get started?

A Discord account and a Neoperro license. Sign in and link your key in the dashboard.

How long does a license last?

Duration depends on the key you purchase. The License tab shows your current expiry.

Where is my config saved?

Saved profiles are associated with your Discord account. You can export a Lua table or download a JSON backup from Config.

Can I reset my device binding?

Eligible licenses can reset the device binding once every 24 hours. Your license stays linked to your Discord account.

Can I share or resell my key?

Keys are for one person. Read the Terms of Service for the sharing and resale policy.

Which games are supported?

Da Strike, Da Uphill, and Da Downhill. Compatibility depends on your runtime, game, and client version.
