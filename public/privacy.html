1. Scope and operator

This policy describes the Neoperro website, dashboard and optional client connector at fosternya.xyz. The service operator uses the name Neoperro. Privacy contact: support@fosternya.xyz.

The application keeps a current config and short-lived connection state. It does not build an activity timeline, count executions, keep play-time charts or collect an account/HWID history. This does not mean that hosting, email or external licensing providers keep no operational records.

3. Cookies and device storage

__Host-neoperro is a necessary session cookie: host-only, Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite=Strict, with a maximum 12-hour lifetime. It is used for workspace access rather than advertising.

neoperro.profile.v1 is a local-storage backup containing your saved profile name, settings and save time. It remains on that browser after logout until you clear it or clear the site’s stored data. Draft edits are also held in page memory. Downloaded exports remain wherever you save them.

No advertising cookies, third-party analytics SDK or session-replay recorder is installed by this application. Website fonts and the eye artwork are served from this site. Hosting security systems can apply their own measures.

4. Why we use the data

We use session and config data to provide the workspace you request, save your chosen settings, link a client, deliver changes and show the current delivery result. Technical checks and rate limits protect availability and prevent unauthorized access. We do not sell application data or use it for targeted advertising.

Where the GDPR or similar law applies, processing necessary to provide the requested service is based on performing that service contract, and proportionate security checks rely on the legitimate interest in protecting users and systems. We use consent where applicable law requires it for an optional feature, and legal obligation where a law requires specific processing. Future optional processing will be explained before it begins.

5. How long data stays

Workspace access expires 12 hours after the session starts; using the site does not extend that deadline. Ending the session deletes the active workspace record and clears its session cookie. Revoking a client connection removes that connection state while leaving the workspace’s config available for the remaining session.

Current connection status is based on a recent heartbeat; a client is shown offline when that lease becomes stale. The stored heartbeat and current revision state are operational state, not a retained sequence of connections or actions.

Expired private-store records are scheduled for deletion by a daily cleanup job. Access is refused immediately after expiry, but physical removal can be later if cleanup fails or has a backlog. Provider storage copies and infrastructure records follow the provider’s retention arrangements. Old application usage aggregates are removed when their workspace is next accessed, or with expiry cleanup.

IP-derived rate counters use short request windows and stay in server-process memory until evicted or the process ends. They are not a persistent usage database. Device backups remain until you clear them. Support correspondence is kept while handling the request and as reasonably needed for applicable legal obligations; the dashboard’s automatic expiry does not delete messages from an email inbox.

6. Who receives information

Vercel hosts this site, APIs and private config storage. Requests expose network information to its infrastructure, and it may process IP addresses, request times, URLs, browser/device metadata, security results and diagnostics. Its operational records are separate from application data. Hosting and delivery can involve processing outside your country. See its Privacy Notice and Data Processing Addendum for its published practices and transfer safeguards.

External license service: running the supplied script loader contacts flowauth.net. Its downloaded authorization code processes your script key and may process device identifiers, IP-derived metadata, execution records and Discord identifiers under that provider’s policy. The current website connector does not send your script key to the Neoperro panel. We cannot promise that an external authorization service retains no records.

Email: messages sent to the contact address pass through the domain’s forwarding provider and the destination mailbox provider. Forwarding and inbox providers process message contents and delivery information. Do not send passwords or connection credentials in a message.

The operator may access workspace data when necessary to maintain the service or address a request. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect legal rights. We do not give public visitors access to other workspaces or account-wide administrative license data.

7. Features that are not collecting data yet

Discord sign-in, verified license linking, a website license vault, automatic account-based connection and HWID/account history are not enabled as of this policy date. A placeholder account area is not a connected Discord identity.

Before sign-in or license linking is enabled, this policy will describe the actual identity fields, credentials, recipients, retention and controls involved. New data collection will not be treated as already authorized just because a planned feature was mentioned here.

8. Your choices and rights

You can use public pages without creating a workspace, choose not to link a client, revoke a connection, end the session, export your config and clear this site’s cookies/local storage in your browser. The browser backup and downloaded exports are under your control. The application does not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability and withdrawal of consent, and the right to complain to your data-protection authority. Contact support@fosternya.xyz to exercise a right. We may request proportionate proof that a workspace or request belongs to you; do not email a password or connection token.

Some technical data is needed to provide a workspace securely. If you decline it, you can still read public pages but may not be able to use private config features. When our practices change, we will update this page and provide any additional notice or choices required by law.
