1. Scope and operator

This page explains what the Neoperro website, dashboard and optional client connector at fosternya.xyz do with your data. We operate under the name Neoperro. For privacy questions, email support@fosternya.xyz.

The panel keeps your current config, Discord session, license association and temporary connection state. It does not keep play-time charts or an account-activity timeline. The licensing service keeps authorization and security records to enforce licenses and investigate key sharing, as explained below.

3. What stays in your browser

__Host-neoperro is the cookie that lets you access your workspace. It expires within 12 hours. It is limited to this host, sent over HTTPS and unavailable to page scripts; its Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite=Strict settings help protect it. It is not used for advertising.

__Host-neoperro_oauth is a ten-minute sign-in cookie with Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax protections. Lax allows the top-level return from Discord. Single-use state and PKCE checks protect that return; the main workspace cookie remains Strict.

neoperro.profile.v1 is a backup in your browser’s local storage. It contains your saved profile name, settings, save time and Discord owner ID so a different signed-in account is not shown that backup. It stays after logout until you clear it or delete this site’s stored data. Unsaved edits stay in page memory. Exported files stay wherever you save them.

We do not install advertising cookies, third-party analytics or session-replay tools. The fonts and eye artwork come from this site. Our hosting provider may use its own security measures.

4. Why we need it

We use this data to sign you in, check and link your license, enforce account and device limits, save your settings, connect your client, send changes and show whether they were applied. Security checks and request limits help keep the service available and prevent unauthorized access or license sharing. We do not sell this data or use it for targeted ads.

Where the GDPR or a similar law applies, we use data needed to provide your requested service on the basis of performing that service contract. Proportionate security checks rely on our legitimate interest in protecting users and systems. We ask for consent when the law requires it for an optional feature, and follow legal obligations when specific processing is required. We will explain any new optional use before it starts.

5. When data is deleted

Your workspace and Discord session expire 12 hours after sign-in. Using them does not extend that time. Ending the session deletes its active server record and clears the session cookie. Revoking a client connection removes the connection state but keeps your config available for the rest of the session.

Your client checks in so the panel can show whether it is connected. If those check-ins stop, the panel shows it as offline. We keep the latest check-in and config revision state, not a list of past connections or actions.

Login and pending pairing state expire after ten minutes. A daily cleanup removes expired private login and workspace records. Access stops at expiry even if physical deletion is delayed by a failed cleanup or backlog. Old usage totals are removed on the next workspace access or expiry cleanup.

The minimal license association and encrypted key remain until you ask us to remove the website record or the service is retired. This is separate from the 12-hour workspace and lets you return to a linked license. Removing the website copy does not transfer or erase the provider’s permanent Discord claim. Provider authorization records, storage copies and infrastructure records follow its own retention rules.

Temporary request counters stay in server memory until evicted or the server process ends. They are not a lasting usage database. Browser backups stay until you clear them. Support messages are kept while handling your request and as reasonably needed for legal obligations. The workspace limit does not delete emails.

6. Who else handles data

Discord handles sign-in and supplies your basic profile after you authorize it. Its CDN receives avatar image requests from the dashboard. See Discord’s Privacy Policy. Your Discord password is not sent to Neoperro.

Vercel hosts this site, APIs and private storage. It receives request information and may process IP addresses, URLs, browser/device metadata, security results and diagnostics. Its infrastructure records are separate from panel data. Processing may take place outside your country. See its Privacy Notice and Data Processing Addendum.

Licensing: the supplied loader contacts flowauth.net. The website also sends a key you enter for lookup and your verified Discord ID when claiming it. The provider handles device binding and authorization. Its security records may include HWID, executor/client information, timestamps, result codes and IP-derived network or approximate location metadata where available. This supports access control and investigation of sharing or resale.

Approximate location is not GPS and can be wrong, especially with VPNs or mobile networks. Showing an approximate country does not mean the provider never processes a raw IP address. Its own privacy and retention rules apply.

License-security records in Neoperro’s administration tools are restricted to the operator. Regular users cannot inspect someone else’s records or blacklist licenses. Providers may still process the underlying information to operate their services. We cannot promise that only one human can ever access provider infrastructure or legally required disclosures.

Email: the domain’s forwarding provider and destination mailbox provider process messages and delivery information when you contact us. Do not send passwords or connection credentials.

We may access workspace data to maintain the service or help with a request, and disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect legal rights. Public visitors cannot access other people’s workspaces or account-wide license administration data.

7. Access decisions and features in progress

The dashboard checks a license before allowing private config and connection actions. Expired, revoked, paused or mismatched licenses are refused. Device-reset cooldowns are enforced by the licensing provider. Status checks may be cached for up to 30 seconds; the script loader remains responsible for runtime authorization.

Suspected sharing or resale may be reviewed by the operator. Confirmed misuse may result in blacklisting. A location change alone is not enough to make that decision. Contact support to ask for a review of a restriction.

Automatic account-based client connection and a dedicated owner security-record screen are still being built. We will describe any additional collection before enabling it. Mentioning a future feature does not authorize unrelated data collection.

8. Your choices and rights

You can read the public pages without opening a workspace. You can leave a client unlinked, revoke a connection, end your session, export your config or clear this site’s cookies and local storage. Browser backups and downloaded exports are under your control. Routine license checks decide whether the panel can be used; contact support if you believe a refusal is wrong.

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may be able to access, correct, delete or move your data, restrict or object to its use, withdraw consent, or complain to your data-protection authority. Email support@fosternya.xyz to make a request. We may ask for reasonable proof that it concerns your workspace or data. Do not email passwords or connection tokens.

Some technical data is necessary to run a secure workspace. If you do not want it processed, you can still read the public pages, but private config features may be unavailable. If our practices change, we will update this page and provide any notice or choices required by law.
